# Changelog

What changed in each version of Second Brain. The same notes are on the [Releases](https://github.com/ChristosLyssas/second-brain/releases) page.

## v2.3.0 · Welcome tour, swipe and your own tabs (6 Nov 2026)

- **Welcome tour** for new users. Replay it any time from Profile.
- **Swipe** left/right on your phone to move between tabs.
- **Your tabs, your way:** reorder or hide tabs from Profile.
- **Move notes around:** hold a note and drag it where you want it.
- **Today:** Layout boxes can now be dragged with your finger too.
- **Calendar:** each event fills the day view for exactly as long as it lasts.
- On phones, a long press no longer selects text by accident.

## v2.2.0 · Calendar events (6 Nov 2026)

- **Your own events:** concerts, appointments, birthdays, with time, location, colour and repeat.
- **Day / Week / Month / Year** views.
- Tap a day to see everything on it in a pop-up, Android-style. Double-tap to add an event.
- Goals open right inside the Calendar, and you can tick their steps there.
- **Notes in a mosaic**, like Google Keep.
- **Today:** a card with this week's events, and a greeting that changes every time.
- **New logo** and a softer light theme.

## v2.1.0 · New look (5 Nov 2026)

- **Clean, consistent design:** new typeface, line icons instead of emoji, soft shadows.
- **Today:** choose which cards you see and in what order.
- **Goals:** a progress ring with a percentage that turns into a ✓ when you're done.
- **Budget:** a more compact overview, one-tap filters, sort by date or amount.
- **Whisperer:** on desktop, capture on the left and notes on the right.
- **"Forgot your password?"** on sign-in, and **delete account** in Profile.

## v2.0.0 · Second Brain 2 (4 Nov 2026)

- **New Today tab:** your day at a glance, with what's urgent, this month's money and your notes.
- **Budget:** month by month, with savings, a savings target, "free to spend" and charts.
- **Goals:** steps you can tick off, with automatic progress. Finance goals follow your savings.
- **Notes:** checklists, links and search. Turn a note into a goal with one tap.
- **Install it on your phone like an app**, on Android and iPhone.
- Export your data (JSON / CSV), language and theme settings.

## v1.1.0 · Calendar and Profile (30 Mar 2026)

- **Calendar** with your goal deadlines.
- **Profile** with your name and photo.
- A new layout across the app.

## v1.0.0 · First release (26 Mar 2026)

- **Goals**, **Whisperer** for quick notes, and **Budget**.
- Sign in with email: your data is stored safely and available on every device.
